# cocomatefoodproducts.github.io

COCOMATE FOOD PRODUCTS. PVT (LTD.) (075 542 8484)

Founded in 1904, New Cocomate Food Products Pvt. Ltd. stands as one of Sri Lanka's most respected and enduring food manufacturing companies, celebrated for its century- long legacy of quality, authenticity, and innovation. The company specializes in producing 100% natural coconut-based products, including Coconut Vinegar and Coconut Treacle, prized for their rich flavor, nutritional value, and purity.

In addition to its core coconut range, the company also manufactures and packages Soya and Noodles under stringent hygiene and safety standards, distributing its products island-wide to households and retailers. Committed to sustainability, New Cocomate extends its eco-conscious philosophy through Seer Plastic Industries, where it recycles plastic into durable, environmentally responsible products.

Guided by over a hundred years of tradition and trust, the company continues to embrace modern technology and ethical practices, ensuring excellence across every stage of production while contributing to Sri Lanka's sustainable agro-food industry.
